# Positioning and Copy

## Brand Position

MELSSY owns the night routine between "je me suis démaquillée" and "je suis prête à dormir". It is not a satin reseller. It is a considered beauty-sleep ritual designed to help women wake up with hair that feels protected, smooth, and ready for the day.

**Promise:** "Votre beauté mérite aussi la nuit."

Premium signals: intentional ritual, composed French, specific product naming, cohesive photography, generous bundle, transparent COD, real order support, and restraint. Never use countdown theater, fake stock numbers, impossible hair claims, "meilleur prix", "promo choc", or generic social-ad language.

## Core Customer

French-speaking Moroccan woman, typically 20-38, city-based, social-media native, beauty-aware, often buying after seeing Meta/TikTok video content. Her friction is not only price: she distrusts anonymous COD sellers, low-quality "satin" claims, confusing delivery, and products that arrive unlike the ad. She wants a small, credible act of self-care that makes mornings easier and feels worthy of her standards.

Use Moroccan-relevant French that is warm and clear. Prefer `DH`, `livraison au Maroc`, `paiement à la livraison`, `votre commande`, `notre équipe`. Avoid slang, France-only logistics references, excessive emojis, and claims that satin treats medical hair/scalp conditions.

## Message Hierarchy

1. Emotional truth: the hours nobody sees still shape how she feels in the morning.
2. Tangible mechanism: smooth satin reduces friction against hair and skin compared with a rougher sleep surface.
3. Ritual proof: coordinated pillowcases, bonnet, scrunchies, and heatless curler turn intention into a repeatable evening gesture.
4. Purchase confidence: one clear bundle, COD, Moroccan support, clear order confirmation and delivery process.

Copy must be edited for final legal, shipping, and returns policy facts. Social proof must be factual and traceable: do not invent review counts, customer names, ratings, locations, deliveries, or scarcity.

Missing content must not render: never expose internal process, pending-content, pre-launch, or placeholder-status copy to shoppers. Rating stars and scarcity elements remain absent until supported by real data.

## Trust Content Required Before Launch

- Authentic UGC/video reviews with written release and exact customer wording.
- Supplier-verified satin fiber, measurements, care instructions, and manufacture facts.
- Published delivery, exchange/return, privacy, terms, and contact information.
- A monitored customer-support channel and documented COD delivery process.
